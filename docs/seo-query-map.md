## Governance

- A query cluster has one primary commercial or editorial destination.
- Filtered search pages and pagination after page one are not index eligible.
- Service detail pages enter the service sitemap only after manual SEO approval.
- New country pages require verified local payment, fulfilment and policy information; thin city or country variants are prohibited.
